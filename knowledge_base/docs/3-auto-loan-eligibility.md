title: Auto Loan Eligibility
---

Auto loans require the applicant to have a steady income and a valid identity and address profile, and the financed vehicle must meet the lender's eligibility checklist. The bank also reviews the applicant's repayment track record and may cap the loan-to-value ratio to keep the credit exposure manageable. Used vehicles often face stricter documentation and age limits than new vehicles.
