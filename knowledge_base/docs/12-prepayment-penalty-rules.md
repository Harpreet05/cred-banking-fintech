title: Prepayment Penalty Rules
---

Prepayment penalties are often charged when a borrower closes a loan before the scheduled tenure ends, especially on fixed-rate or special offers. Some products allow partial prepayments without a penalty, while others restrict the amount or frequency of such payments. Customers should check the sanction letter for exact prepayment rules and any lock-in period before making a lump-sum payment.
