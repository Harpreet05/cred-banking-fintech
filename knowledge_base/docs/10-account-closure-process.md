title: Account Closure Process
---

Closing an account usually requires the customer to submit a request, clear any pending dues, and settle outstanding charges or minimum-balance shortfalls. The bank may issue a final statement after closure and can require specific documentation if the account is jointly held or if there is a loan-linked relation in the same profile. Accounts with recurring debits or active lockers may need extra clearance before the account can be fully closed.
