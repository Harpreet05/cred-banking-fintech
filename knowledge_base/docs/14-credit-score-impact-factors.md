title: Credit Score Impact Factors
---

Credit score is shaped by repayment consistency, utilization balance, credit age, and the number of recent credit enquiries. Late payments and defaults can reduce the score materially, while consistent, on-time repayment over a long period improves the borrower's profile. Credit score is only one part of the underwriting decision, but it has a strong influence on approval and pricing.
