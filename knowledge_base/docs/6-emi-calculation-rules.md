title: EMI Calculation Rules
---

EMI is usually calculated using the principal amount, the applicable interest rate, and the loan tenure. For reducing-balance loans, interest is charged on the outstanding principal, so early EMIs are more interest-heavy and later EMIs are more principal-heavy. Changes in rate type, fees, or repayment frequency can alter the monthly EMI even when the total loan amount remains the same.
