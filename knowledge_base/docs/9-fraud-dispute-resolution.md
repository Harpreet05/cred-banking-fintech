title: Fraud Dispute Resolution
---

Fraud disputes are handled through a structured review that begins when the customer reports an unauthorized or suspicious transaction. The bank verifies the transaction timeline, checks whether the account holder or a third party approved the action, and may place a temporary block while the review is underway. If fraud is confirmed, the bank may reverse the loss, update the account controls, and document the case for compliance and future audit review.
